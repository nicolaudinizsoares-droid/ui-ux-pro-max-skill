## Claim rules (read before writing any copy)

- **Never lead with collagen quantity.** 12 mg is far below competitors (Lemme: 200 mg). Mention
  collagen only as an ingredient, never as a hero or a number.
- **Supplement claims stay structure / function:** "supports", "helps maintain". No "grows hair",
  "stops hair loss", "cures", or any disease / condition claim.
- **Meta personal attributes policy:** never assert or imply the reader has a condition
  ("Is your hair falling out?", "Your thinning hair..."). Speak about the situation in third person
  or first person ("Nails that used to peel..."), not "you have X".
- **Meta before / after:** no side-by-side body or hair before / after photos implying results.
  Before / after of *objects* or routines (e.g. a cluttered shelf of pills → one bottle) is fine.
- No invented timelines ("results in 2 weeks") or statistics. Only numbers from the label.
- High-dose biotin can skew some lab tests (FDA safety communication). Never claim "safe for
  everyone" or "no side effects".
